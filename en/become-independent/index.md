<h2>Why this is not a "de-Google-your-life" course</h2>
<p>Digital sovereignty doesn't mean giving up everything. It means deciding consciously what you entrust to whom — and having an exit for every service. Using Signal but Google Maps doesn't make you a traitor to the cause. But handing your entire communication, documents, payments and movements to one company means you no longer have a decision.</p>
