## Rules for inventory pieces

1. **Every piece gets a number** (`D-<case>-<piece>`) and a description with date and source location.
2. **No piece without a source.** Whoever submits an image links the original location (court, authority, press, video mirror).
3. **Copyright:** submitted images need a reproduction basis (official work, quotation right for reporting, explicit license). Third-party photos only with source and caption — never without origin.
4. **No montages.** Edited images must be marked as such; screenshots show date and URL.
5. **Additions** go through pull requests (see [Contribute](/en/contribute/)).

## Placeholders — and what they mean

The current cards are **typographic reference cards** of the wiki (not reproductions of originals). They show each piece's number, case, core facts and source — until the community submits licensable originals. That is deliberate: we link originals at their sources instead of copying them without legal basis. If you can contribute a rights-cleared or licensed original, replace the card via a pull request — with source attribution.
