# Journalists killed: the state record

<p class="lead">"Which state killed how many journalists?" can only be answered cleanly for the period since **1992**, when the Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ) began its systematic database with perpetrator attribution. Before that: documented episodes, no comparable statistics. We show both — separately.</p>

<div class="meta-line"><span class="tag red">Method</span> CPJ database (1992–2026), attribution "Government/Military Officials" — plus documented historical episodes. No extrapolations.</div>

### What the numbers mean — and don't

**Israel/OPT (210)** leads by a wide margin. The 2023–2026 Gaza war dominates: 191 of the 210 deaths since October 2023. CPJ's February 2026 special report: Israel was responsible for **two-thirds** of all journalists killed worldwide in 2025.

**A "0" is not an endorsement:** Germany, France (in this attribution), Spain, Portugal have killed no journalists via state agents since 1992 (per CPJ). That does not make them surveillance-free — only non-killing. The [country index](/en/countries/) shows the other dimensions.

## Part 2: before 1992 — documented episodes

<p>For the pre-1992 era there is <strong>no comparable systematic statistics</strong>. Anyone selling you a table "170 journalists killed by state X, 1934–1945" invented it or extrapolated from single cases — unusable either way. What is documented:</p>

- **Germany 1933–1945**: resistance journalists arrested, deported, murdered — individual cases documented (Gedenkstätte Deutscher Widerstand), no modern-standard total.
- **USSR 1917–1991**: the Stalin terror hit journalists (e.g. writer Isaac Babel, shot 1940); archive numbers, no unified CPJ-standard count.
- **Latin America 1960s–1980s**: military dictatorships in Argentina (desaparecidos including journalists), Chile (1973 coup), Brazil (military regime). Documented cases, no total statistics.
- **Spain 1936–1975**: civil war and Franco era — journalists murdered on both sides; systematic accounting incomplete.

**The finding:** states have always killed journalists. But the precise, comparable count began in 1992 with CPJ. Everything before is memory work, not statistics. Anyone claiming a "100-year exact table" is lying — to you and to themselves.

## Debunking

**False:** "Israel killed the most journalists of all time." — In the CPJ era (1992–2026) Israel holds the highest state-attributed count (210). "Of all time" — there are no data. Two different claims.

**Also false:** "Only 1 journalist killed by US government agents, so the US is harmless." — The number counts deaths on US soil. It does not count deaths via US arms exports, US drones, or allied governments. It is an indicator, not a morality meter.

## Open questions

- How many press workers died in Gaza whom CPJ did not classify as "journalists"? CPJ itself calls its number a "lower bound".
- No Israeli proceeding has to date (10/2026) produced a conviction over the 210 cases.
