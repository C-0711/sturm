## How to read this table

**A state that kills journalists is the worst case.** Israel/OPT leads CPJ's attribution of journalists killed by government/military officials by a wide margin — 210 of 1992–2026, 191 of them since October 2023 (Gaza war). That is not an opinion index; those are bodies.

**But beware simple rankings:** RSF measures working conditions, not a state's morality. France (rank 25) has a video-surveillance problem (VSA) and an anti-influence law, but zero journalists killed by state agents. Israel (116) has 210. The index measures "where can journalists work?" — a different question that must also be asked.

## What this index is NOT

- Not our own scoring. We aggregate existing measurements; their methodology belongs to their publishers.
- No weighting. A country with 0 killed journalists can still be a surveillance state (China jails rather than kills accredited journalists).
- Not exhaustive. "Killed by state agents" covers confirmed attribution only.
