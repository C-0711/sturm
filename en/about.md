# About STURM

<p class="lead">STURM is an open, collaborative wiki against censorship, for digital sovereignty, and against control by corporations and states. Community-run; initiated by a person who goes by "Mastermind".</p>

## Why this wiki exists

Censorship today rarely works with censors. It works with terms of service, algorithmic feeds, platform bans, SLAPP suits, double-titled laws, and the fear that spreads when examples leak through. This wiki documents these examples — **with sources**. Not to shock, but to be a tool: for debates, research, and your own decisions about whom to trust.

## Principles

1. **Evidence over claims.** Every factual statement carries a source. No source, no sentence.
2. **Debunk all lies — including our own.** Every dossier has a "Debunking" section correcting oversimplifications, including those convenient to our own position.
3. **No electioneering.** Cases from all political directions; documented censorship from left, right, state, private.
4. **Anonymous initiation.** The initiator goes by "Mastermind" — no name, no link, no organisation. The wiki should be judged on its arguments and evidence, not on its founder.

## Transparency

- **Code and content:** [github.com/C-0711/sturm](https://github.com/C-0711/sturm) — every change visible in Git history.
- **Licence:** content CC BY-SA 4.0 (see [Licence](/en/license/)).
- **Funding:** none. No sponsor, no donations, no ads. The domain and hosting costs are carried privately.
- **Editorial:** no newsroom — maintainers and contributors via pull requests; disputes in [Discussions](https://github.com/C-0711/sturm/discussions).

## How it's built

- **Tech:** Jekyll on GitHub Pages — static, no trackers, no cookies, no external fonts/CDNs.
- **Languages:** German and English are working languages; French, Spanish, Portuguese are on the way ([help translate](/en/contribute/)).
- **Article standard:** summary → evidence → timeline → debunking → open questions → sources. Template in [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/C-0711/sturm/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
