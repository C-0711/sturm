# "50,000 reais a day": fining users, not just platforms

<p class="lead">Alongside the X blockade, Justice Moraes threatened the users: anyone reaching X via VPN risked daily fines — reported internationally as ~US$8,900 per day. A legal tool became a punishable act.</p>

<div class="meta-line"><span class="tag">Country</span> Brazil <span class="tag">Topic</span> VPN fines <span class="tag red">Status</span> Order documented</div>

## Summary

As part of the 30.08.2024 X blockade, Moraes threatened fines against users circumventing the block via VPN — reported by the LA Times as US$8,900/day. Whether individual VPN users were ultimately fined is not documented (as of 10/2026); the order and its deterrent effect are. A state that criminalises circumventing a platform block — while the block's own legality was contested — shifts the pressure from the company to every single citizen.
