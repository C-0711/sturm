# Brazil: one judge blocks X for 22 million people

<p class="lead">From 30 August to 8 October 2024, X was blocked across Brazil — ordered by a single Supreme Court justice, Alexandre de Moraes. The price of lifting it: millions in fines, blocked accounts, a legal representative. And VPN users faced daily fines of up to 50,000 reais.</p>

<div class="meta-line"><span class="tag">Country</span> Brazil <span class="tag">Topic</span> Platform block <span class="tag red">Status</span> Lifted after 40 days; fines paid</div>

## Summary

After Elon Musk refused to block accounts targeted by Moraes' "digital militia" investigations, the justice ordered a nationwide block on 30.08.2024. When X did not pay fines or name a representative, Moraes froze Starlink assets and the government collected R$18.35M from X/Starlink. After X paid ~R$28M (≈US$5.2M), named a representative and blocked the accounts, the block was lifted on 8 October 2024. One judge decided the speech access of 22 million Brazilians — in fast-track proceedings with no effective appeal against him.

## Timeline

- **30.08.2024** — nationwide block ordered; R$18.3M in fines outstanding
- **13.09.2024** — Starlink accounts frozen; R$18.35M collected
- **08.10.2024** — block lifted: fines paid (~R$28M total), representative named, accounts blocked

## Debunking

**False:** "Musk won." — X paid the fines, named the representative, and blocked the accounts. The blockade ended through compliance, not vindication.
