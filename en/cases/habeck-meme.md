# Police raid a pensioner's home over a "blockhead" meme

<p class="lead">A 64-year-old Bavarian shared a meme calling Economy Minister Robert Habeck a "Schwachkopf" (blockhead). Habeck filed charges. Police searched his house. The case became the shorthand for Germany's debate over insult law online.</p>

<div class="meta-line"><span class="tag">Country</span> Germany <span class="tag">Topic</span> § 185 StGB insult <span class="tag red">Status</span> Proceedings ended by defendant's death</div>

## Summary

In spring 2024 Stefan Niehoff posted the meme on X. Habeck filed a criminal complaint. On 5 November 2024 police searched Niehoff's home in Burgpreppach — the warrant cited further posts including one under § 130 (incitement). Bamberg district court later issued a penalty order (90 day-fines) concerning a different post; the insult proceedings were finally closed in October 2025 after Niehoff died on 31 May 2025, aged 65.

## Debunking

**False:** "Police raided a home over one meme." — The raid covered several posts, including an incitement allegation. But the fact that a "blockhead" meme was part of a search warrant is exactly why the case ignited the debate over § 185 and proportionality.
