# Algorithmic video surveillance: from "Olympic test" to permanence

<p class="lead">For the 2024 Paris Olympics, France legalised AI-assisted video surveillance "experimentally". The experiment was extended — to the end of 2027, with the Senate voting for 2030. Exception becomes the rule.</p>

<div class="meta-line"><span class="tag">Country</span> France <span class="tag">Topic</span> Biometric mass surveillance <span class="tag red">Status</span> Extended (2027; Senate: 2030)</div>

## Summary

Article 10 of the 2023 Olympic law (Loi JOP) permitted "algorithmic video surveillance" (VSA) for the Games. In February 2025 an amendment extended it to 31 December 2027 via the 2030 Winter Olympics bill; the Senate voted an extension to 2030. Privacy International and Amnesty France criticise the scheme as biometric mass capture incompatible with the GDPR.

## Timeline

- **2023** — Loi JOP legalises VSA as a time-limited "experiment"
- **11.02.2025** — extension to 31.12.2027 adopted
- **2025** — Senate votes for extension to 2030; final text pending

## Debunking

**False:** "It was just an experiment for the Olympics." — The "time limit" has been extended twice. "Experiment" is a euphemism for permanence-by-installment.
