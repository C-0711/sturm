# The revolving door: from the CNN press office to the White House — and back

<p class="lead">Journalists become administration spokespeople; officials become on-air "analysts". The personnel churnal between US politics and journalism is everyday life. Documented cases instead of assertions.</p>

## Summary

The US government–media revolving door is biography, not theory: Jay Carney (Time → White House press office → Amazon), George Stephanopoulos (Clinton administration → ABC News), Jen Psaki (White House press secretary → MSNBC), Nicolle Wallace (Bush administration → MSNBC), and dozens more per administration. The consequence: news and politics share personnel, relationships, and perspectives. This is not proof of "control" — it is a documented structural conflict of interest.

## Debunking

**False:** "All journalists are agents." — Nonsense. The door is a structural problem (networks, conflicts, perspectives), not proof of indoctrination.

**Also false:** "Every democracy has this." — The scale of the US churnal (dozens per administration, both parties) is exceptional — and being bipartisan doesn't make it harmless.
