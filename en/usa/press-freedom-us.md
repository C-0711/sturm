# Press freedom in the US: from "satisfactory" to "difficult"

<p class="lead">In RSF's 2026 index the US fell seven places — to rank 64. Political violence against journalists, the dismantling of USAGM (Voice of America, Radio Free Europe), and a president's systematic attacks on the press. Numbers over opinions.</p>

<div class="meta-line"><span class="tag">Index</span> RSF WPFI 2026 <span class="tag red">Rank</span> 64/180</div>

## Summary

The RSF index measures five indicators (political, economic, legal, socio-cultural, security). US 2026: **rank 64**, 62.61 points, down seven. RSF's stated drivers: the president's systematic hostility to the press, mass firings at the US Agency for Global Media with global fallout for VOA/RFE/RL/RFA, the detention and deportation of journalist Mario Guevara, police violence at protests (Minnesota), and budget cuts to public media.

## Debunking

**False:** "There is no censorship in the US." — The index measures how freely journalists can actually work, not whether a censor board exists. Rank 64 — behind Jamaica and OECS, ahead of Panama — says the practice worsened, whatever the First Amendment says.

**Also false:** "Trump destroyed press freedom." — The decline predates Trump's second term (2025: rank 57). But the 2026 drivers are overwhelmingly this administration's measures.
