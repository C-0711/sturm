# Lobbying: money buys access — and policy

<p class="lead">In 2024 corporations and associations spent an estimated **$4.6 billion** on lobbying — a record. How money shapes politics: from legality through grey zones to the red line where democracy tips into oligarchy.</p>

<div class="meta-line"><span class="tag">Data</span> OpenSecrets / LDA database <span class="tag red">Year</span> 2024</div>

## Summary

The Lobbying Disclosure Act forces disclosure; OpenSecrets aggregates. 2024: over $4.6 billion (record). Top spenders: tech, pharma, finance, defense. Money buys no bill — it buys **access**: meetings, attention, priority. Gilens &amp; Page (2014) found that when economic elites and business interests favour a policy, its odds rise dramatically; average citizens' preferences have near-zero effect.

## Debunking

**False:** "Every politician is bought." — Provable causality is rare. Provable: structural access advantage. Money buys probability, not certainty — but probability is power.
