# Book bans: 10,046 instances in one school year

<p class="lead">PEN America counted **10,046** individual book bans in US schools in 2023/24 — 4,231 unique titles. Since 2021: nearly 34,000. What a "ban" is, how they happen, and what the debate reveals.</p>

<div class="meta-line"><span class="tag">Data</span> PEN America <span class="tag red">Period</span> School year 2023/24</div>

## Summary

PEN America's "Banned in the USA" series has documented school book bans since 2021: 10,046 instances in 2023/24 alone; ~34,000 since 2021 — disproportionately affecting books on LGBTQ+ themes, racism, and race. Two forms: formal removals by school boards and informal "soft removals" by teachers pre-empting complaints.

## Debunking

**False:** "It's just a few angry parents." — PEN documents organized campaigns providing complaint templates; bans concentrate in few districts.

**Also false:** "Only pedagogically dubious books are removed." — The most-banned titles are predominantly literary, award-winning works with race and LGBTQ+ content. "Dubious" is a political category, not a literary one.
