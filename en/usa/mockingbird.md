# "The CIA and the Media": Carl Bernstein, 400 journalists, one scandal

<p class="lead">In 1977 Carl Bernstein (of Watergate) documented in Rolling Stone: at least 400 American journalists worked for the CIA during the Cold War — from ABC to the New York Times to CBS News. "Operation Mockingbird" as a name is legend; the cooperation is documented.</p>

<div class="meta-line"><span class="tag">Year</span> 1977 <span class="tag">Source</span> Rolling Stone, 20.10.1977 <span class="tag red">Status</span> Documented</div>

## Summary

Bernstein's article, based on CIA documents, officials and editors' memories: at least **400** journalists served the Agency between the 1950s and 1970s — as informants, couriers, placement platforms. The Church Committee (1975/76) had exposed the foundations; in 1977 CIA Director Stansfield Turner told Congress the practice would end. "Mockingbird" as an operation name appears in **no** document — it comes from later, partly speculative accounts. The facts are documented; the legend is not.

## Debunking

**False:** "The CIA controls the media to this day." — Bernstein documented cooperation to 1977, when Congress stopped it. Everything beyond is assertion without evidence.

**Also false:** "It's a conspiracy theory." — Bernstein is the Watergate reporter; the basis is the Church Committee and CIA documents. Denying documented history makes you unbelievable, not the history.
