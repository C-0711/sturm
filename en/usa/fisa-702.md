# FISA 702: 278,000 improper FBI searches — renewed anyway

<p class="lead">Section 702 allows spying on foreigners abroad — but Americans' communications get swept in. The FBI improperly searched Americans' data 278,000 times in two years. Congress renewed the program anyway — until April 2026.</p>

<div class="meta-line"><span class="tag">Law</span> RISAA, Pub. L. 118-49 <span class="tag red">Sunset</span> 20 April 2026</div>

## How it works

Section 702 lets the NSA compel providers to intercept targets abroad — without individual warrants. Collateral: Americans' communications with those targets are captured and become searchable. The FBI may search this "incidentally collected" data.

## The numbers

- **278,000** improper FBI queries of US persons (Dec 2019–Nov 2020), including searches of activists, journalists, congressional staff — revealed in FISC Judge Boasberg's opinion (April 2022, declassified 2023).
- **20.04.2024** — Congress renews Section 702 for two years via RISAA — without the demanded warrant requirement.
- **20.04.2026** — next sunset.

## Debunking

**False:** "FISA 702 only targets foreigners." — That is the letter of the law; the practice sweeps in Americans. The FISC itself spoke of "widespread violations".
