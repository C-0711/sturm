# Our Mission

<p class="lead">Freedom is not a state. Freedom is a practice — and it begins with no one unchallenged deciding what you may say, read, and know. STURM exists to arm that practice with evidence.</p>

## What we want

**1. Make censorship visible — with evidence, not assertion.**
Censorship is rarely called censorship anymore. It goes by "content moderation", "policy violation", "countering disinformation", "proportionality". We document what actually happened: court rulings, legislative bills, leaked internal directives, dated primary sources. Every case in this wiki is a dossier you can verify — sentence by sentence, footnote by footnote.

**2. Make sovereignty possible — with instructions, not sermons.**
"Become independent!" is cheap when nobody explains how. Our guide shows step by step: Signal over WhatsApp, Firefox over Chrome, your own domain over platform accounts, keep cash, use pseudonyms as a right. Every recommendation names its price (time, comfort, money) so you decide which independence is worth it for you.

**3. Debunk all lies — including our own.**
The most discrediting thing about most "freedom" projects is that they only correct the other side's lies. We make the opposite mandatory: every dossier contains a "Debunking" section correcting oversimplifications — **including the ones that would help our own cause.** Whoever fights censorship with lies loses the only thing that beats censorship: the truth.

**4. Lay open the statistics of unfreedom.**
Press freedom, democracy, journalists killed — serious measurements exist (RSF, V-Dem, CPJ). We invent no scores of our own and mix in no opinions. We show the numbers, name the sources, and explain what they do **not** say.

## What we are not

- **No party mouthpiece.** Censorship from left, right, state, private — all of it appears, if documented. Whoever only records the other side's censorship is campaigning, not doing truth work.
- **No haven for conspiracy lore.** "Show the emails, the court order, the investigative report" — without evidence, nothing. This strictness is not a weakness; it is the product.
- **No founder cult.** The initiator goes by "Mastermind" — deliberately nameless and unlinked. This wiki is judged on its arguments, not its founder.

## Why now?

Because fundamental rights are under pressure everywhere at once: platform bans in Brazil,[^1] deletion mandates in Europe,[^2] double-titled laws in France,[^3] mass surveillance in the US,[^4] journalists killed at record rates in 2025.[^5] And because nearly every one of these examples is known individually but nowhere collected **reliably, multilingually, and verifiably**. That is the gap STURM closes.

## How to contribute

STURM is a wiki: anyone can correct errors, add cases, translate. The path runs through [issues, pull requests and discussions on GitHub](https://github.com/C-0711/sturm) — open, versioned, comprehensible to all. The standard for every contribution is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/C-0711/sturm/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). Three merged dossiers qualify you as a maintainer.

## Our technology — and why it is part of the mission

<p class="lead">A site that writes against datakraken while loading trackers has not understood its own subject. STURM is built the way we recommend.</p>

### Static instead of data-hungry

STURM is a **static site** (Jekyll, built on GitHub Pages). Concretely:

| Practically every website | STURM |
|---|---|
| Tracking pixels, analytics scripts, cookies | **0 trackers, 0 cookies** — there is simply no script that could watch you |
| External fonts (Google Fonts) — report you on every page view | **0 external calls** — system fonts, everything served from our own repository |
| Server-side profiles, databases of user behaviour | **No database, no login** — the site does not know its visitors |
| Ad CDNs sharing movement profiles with third parties | **No ads, no CDN calls** — one CSS file, inline, done |

This site cannot surveil you — **not because we promise it, but because the architecture does not allow it.**

### A public repository instead of a black box

All code and content live [publicly on GitHub](https://github.com/C-0711/sturm): every change visible in Git history, every source checkable, every correction traceable. There is no back-room editorial — contributions run through pull requests anyone can read.

### Footnotes instead of "everybody knows"

Every factual statement carries its source in scholarly format (author, title, date, URL, access date) — as an in-text kramdown footnote and in the source list at the end of each article. It is deliberately as rigorous as a dissertation: **whoever disagrees with us should be able to disagree at the sources.**

### Copyable instead of fenced in

All content is licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0** — anyone may mirror, translate, print, and spread it offline. A site against censorship that hid behind walls would contradict itself. If sturm.wtf ever disappears, the corpus lives on in every fork.
